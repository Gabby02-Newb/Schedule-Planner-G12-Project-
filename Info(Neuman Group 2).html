What is the Topic About?

  • Effectiveness of web-based schedule planning for student time management.
  • Schedule planner definition: A system that organizes and allocates time for specific tasks or subjects.
  • Popular examples: Google Calendar, Notion, Trello, MyStudyLife.
  • Key features: Reminders, color coding, time blocking, and integration tools.
  • Benefits achieved: Improved prioritization, better routines, and increased productivity.

Main Topic

  • Definition: A comprehensive system used to organize time and manage tasks effectively.
  • Primary purpose: Improve time management, boost productivity, reduce stress, and track goals.
  • Various types: Manual planners, digital apps, academic planners, work planners.
  • Key features included: Time blocking, reminders, sync, automatic updates.
  • Main benefits: Routine building, focus enhancement, long-term planning, work-life balance.

Key Focus Areas

  • Online accessibility for seamless task management from anywhere.
  • Prioritization methods: Eisenhower Matrix, ABC analysis, MoSCoW method.
  • Multiple viewing options: Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly perspectives.
  • Integration capabilities: Works with other productivity tools and platforms.
  • User experience: Intuitive, customizable, and visually appealing interface.

Core Objectives

  • Measure effectiveness of the planner in improving student time management.
  • Combat procrastination and increase overall productivity levels.
  • Promote goal-oriented planning and strategic decision-making.
  • Support balance between academic responsibilities and personal life.
  • Provide tools for visual scheduling and stress reduction.
